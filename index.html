The dress code

Three color moments. One unforgettable weekend.

Friday calls for soft pink — blush, dusty rose, mauve, and baby pink in silhouettes that go from dinner to villa without skipping a beat. Saturday day is Something Blue: powder blue, cornflower, denim, linen sets, and sun-ready sundresses built for walking the Quarter. Saturday night is full glam — sequins, sparkle, after-dark energy. Sunday is free. Wear whatever survived Saturday, or call back to pink or blue if you're feeling sentimental.

Friday night / Soft Pink

Land in something easy but still elevated: a blush, dusty rose, or mauve midi with delicate jewelry and heels you can actually stand in after the flight. The energy is soft glamour for dinner at Shaya, then a quick switch into villa-comfy pink for game night later.

Money

What costs are shared?

Rushali is covering the Airbnb. Expect food, drinks, airport rides, local rides, activities, and late-night snacks to be split amongst the group unless someone calls out a treat.

Reservations

Which bookings are fixed?

Keep Friday dinner, Saturday brunch, Saturday dinner, Saturday's 6:00 PM ghost tour, and Sunday's 12:45 PM brunch protected on your calendar. We will move around the city, but reservations anchor the day.

Packing

What should I not forget?

Photo ID, a small going-out bag, portable charger, blister bandaids, pajamas, sunscreen, sunglasses, a refillable water bottle, a swimsuit for the pool and hot tub, and one outfit that makes you feel expensive.

Photos

Where do photos go?

Drop favorites and unhinged candids into the group chat within 24 hours. Ask before posting close-ups; assume Rushali gets first approval on bride-forward portraits.

Mobility

Can I wear heels?

Choose block heels, platforms, boots, flats, or a backup pair you can fold into your bag. Cobblestones and stilettos are not a love story.

The moodboard

Balconies, brass, powdered sugar, after-dark streets.

A little destination inspiration before the photo dump gets personal. Save the pretty establishing shots; the ridiculous ones will find us naturally.
